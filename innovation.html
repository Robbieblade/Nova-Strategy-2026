What Innovation means at Nova

Learn. Improve. Share.

Innovation is how Nova keeps getting better. We are a learning organisation: everyone, whatever their role, develops their expertise and never stops learning. We improve, sharpening practice through evidence, coaching and a shared language for teaching, and we use technology and AI with purpose, to do more with less rather than more for its own sake. We share, so that good practice travels between schools and nobody improves alone.

Learn, improve, share. Innovation is a driver, not a scoreboard: we grow it through professional development, quality assurance and the spread of what works, and we judge it by the results it produces, not by counting courses attended.

Where this sits

Curriculum, teaching, leadership and governance

Innovation speaks to the Ofsted areas of curriculum & teaching and leadership & governance, and delivers the DfE school improvement and workforce pillars. It is also how autonomy becomes powerful rather than isolating, because nobody solves problems alone.
