What Teamwork means at Nova

Attend. Contribute. Belong.

Teamwork is how Nova builds the culture, relationships and systems that let people and schools thrive. It starts with attending: pupils here every day, adults consistent and reliable, because being present is the first condition of everything that follows. It builds through how we contribute: everyone adding something to their team and their community, collaborating across schools rather than solving problems alone. And it deepens into belonging: every child safe, known and included, and every colleague valued and part of the Nova family.

Attend, contribute, belong. These are not slogans but the daily habits that make a school somewhere people want to be, and they are what makes the rest of our work possible.

Where this sits

Inclusion, attendance and behaviour

Teamwork speaks directly to the Ofsted areas of inclusion and attendance & behaviour, and to the inclusion thread now running through every evaluation area. It underpins the DfE workforce and governance pillars.
