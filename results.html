What Results means at Nova

Achieve. Develop. Flourish.

Results are the point of all of it. Every child should achieve highly, in reading, writing, maths and across the curriculum, with the gaps that hold some children back closing year on year. Every child should develop fully as a person, through character, wellbeing and the whole-child entitlement we promise and can evidence. And every child should flourish beyond us, leaving ready for the next stage with the knowledge, confidence and positive destination that opens doors.

Achieve, develop, flourish. Results are what we commit to and what we report, and because every school and every child is different, we measure them by context, honestly and ambitiously.

Accountability

Measured fairly, by context

Attainment, progress, attendance and destinations are set as realistic, ambitious targets for each school, informed by its context and similar schools nationally.
